Hannah Rose Widmer has built an international modelling career defined by versatility, elegance, and authenticity. Born and raised in a small town, she has proven that global success can begin in the most unexpected places. Today, her work spans luxury editorials, beauty campaigns, commercial advertising, and high-fashion publications, earning recognition across Europe and the United States.

body: >-


Beyond modelling, Widmer is an artist, equestrian, and advocate for creativity and service. With a background in Art Therapy and a passion for watercolour, stained glass, pottery, and sculpture, she brings depth and originality to every project. She is also expanding her creative portfolio as an author and is currently working on her debut novel, which is expected to be released later this year. Beyond fashion, she is honoured to be featured on the cover of Schön! Switzerland, a particularly meaningful milestone as Switzerland reflects her family heritage. Her ability to move seamlessly between high fashion, the arts, and commercial work has established her as one of the industry’s most versatile rising talents while staying true to the humility and values shaped by her small town roots.


Widmer has appeared on the covers and pages of leading publications including Vogue, Numéro, Harper’s Bazaar, L’Officiel, Marie Claire, Glamour, Maxim, and Grazia. She was featured on the cover of Vogue Czechoslovakia Travel Issue, appeared on the back cover of Vogue Mexico Beauty as the face of luxury fashion brand Solangel, and fronted the 2026 Capulet Fine Jewellery campaign featured by Vogue Italia. Her career also includes campaigns, Times Square billboards, television appearances, and collaborations with internationally recognized photographers, designers, and luxury brands.
