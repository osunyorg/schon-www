Issue 04 – in motion
For the second year, Kim Jones, the Artistic Director of Dior Men's line, has collaborated with Parley for the Oceans, a leading environmental organization focused on ocean conservation. This alliance has given birth to a beach capsule collection that mirrors Dior's drive for sustainable and innovative fashion, and reflects the founding designer's passion for the sea, flowers, and gardens.

body: >-


words. Frank Herbrand photos. Till Janz + Alfredo Piola issue 4 – in motion


The collection, characterized by an exuberant color palette and iconic motifs, is made of 96% recycled materials, reflecting Jones’ commitment to sustainability. It includes pieces like surf suits and stoles adorned with an underwater photo print, symbolizing the beauty of the maritime universe.


Moreover, Dior unveils a meticulously crafted surfboard as a testament to their dedication to craftsmanship and innovation. Created in partnership with Notox for the Fall 2023 line, the eco-friendly surfboard is a technical marvel modeled in 3D, blending precision and performance.


Embodying Dior’s ethos, the surfboard, embellished with the Dior signature motif, celebrates the alluring beauty of the aquatic world, serving as a tribute to the thrill of exploration. words. Frank Herbrand photos. Till Janz + Alfredo Piola
