Issue 02 – May 23 – welcome to the New
Opacity is a fashion shoot that mixes retro, punk, and high fashion styles, showcasing the shapes and colors of the clothes. The name "Opacity" was chosen for this shoot because of the soft filter used during the photography process, which created a dreamy and slightly blurred effect in the images. This effect adds a layer of opacity or haze to the photos, enhancing the depth and artistic quality of the shoot. Plus, I used mixed lighting to create a smooth effect that enhances the overall look of the shoot. The result is a super mesmerizing blend of fashion, art, and style that's sure to capture your attention and inspire your creativity.
