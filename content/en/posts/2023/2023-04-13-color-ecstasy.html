Issue 01 – FEB/MAR 23 – In Love with Switzerland
"Color ecstasy" is a term that suggests an overwhelming experience of color, often associated with intense joy, excitement, or pleasure. It can describe a state of mind where one feels immersed in vibrant hues, and experiences a heightened sense of sensory pleasure or stimulation. In art, color ecstasy can be achieved through the use of bold, bright, or contrasting colors that create a strong emotional impact on the viewer.
