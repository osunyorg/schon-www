Issue 02 – May 23 – welcome to the New
Æschne is a fascinating creature that captures the imagination of all who encounter it. It is a creature of duality, capable of flying in the opposite direction, confounding our expectations. Its shimmering shapes inspire a sense of wonder and magic in all who see it. For some, the sound of its vibrating wings is a lullaby, a reminder of the peace that left her country. But Aeschne is elusive, fleeting, and can disappear in an instant, leaving us wondering why we are still thinking about it. Perhaps it is because of the needed escape we are seeking.
