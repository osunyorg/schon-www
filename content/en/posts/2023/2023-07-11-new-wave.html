Issue 03 - the water issue
We wanted to capture movement and power of water but also tranquility and serenity of it. We like that It looked like a splash of water frozen in time. Model's hair dripping wet, the glistening sparkle on her skin and the subtle reflection in her eyes - all of it giving a sense of purity and freshness. The images are timeless, ethereal, and sensual, inviting the viewers to appreciate the artistry and beauty of water in a new light.
