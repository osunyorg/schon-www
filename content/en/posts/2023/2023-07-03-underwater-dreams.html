Issue 03 - the water issue
The fashion editorial is inspired by the mysterious and mesmerizing world of aquatic creatures. The concept revolves around creating an abstract underwater world, featuring a new type of creature that is a combination of various underwater organisms and humans. The female host is depicted as a stunningly beautiful and surreal creature with jellyfish tentacles, sharp barbs, bubble - like clothes, and gills to breathe underwater. The imagery aims to portray a dreamy and abs tract feel with a calm and melancholic mood. The overall color scheme is centered around a dominant blue theme, enriched with purple hues to evoke an underwater ambiance. This fashion editorial is a breathtaking interpretation of combining various aquatic organisms and creating a surreal yet bewitching world of fashion.
