Issue 02 – May 23 – welcome to the New
Fashion’s ultimate suit of armour; leather and sheen is truly having its moment as we swing into 2023. Synonymous with a classic and timeless look, this style is also tapping into a new breed of cool girl dominance- defining itself as villain- era chic. An unexpected style pairing for what started as a beauty story, yet creates an effective and dramatic framing to the makeup & hair. Mirroring the sleek, metallic inspired trends of the season. Featuring a mixture of worn- in leather, boxy moto silhouettes and eye- catching hardware elements. The works "cold" and "sleek" come to mind- expressed through every element of this story be it silhouettes, accessories, hair or beauty. The perfect fashion story straddling the months between Spring and Summer.
