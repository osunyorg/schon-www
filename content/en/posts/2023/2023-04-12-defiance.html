Issue 01 – FEB/MAR 23 – In Love with Switzerland
Defiance is a behavior or attitude that involves openly resisting or opposing authority, rules, or social norms. It is characterized by a willingness to stand up for one's beliefs, even in the face of opposition or punishment. Defiance can be seen as a form of rebellion against established systems, values, or expectations. People who exhibit defiance may do so for a variety of reasons, such as a desire for autonomy or a need to express individuality. Defiance can manifest in different ways, ranging from passive resistance to active disobedience.
