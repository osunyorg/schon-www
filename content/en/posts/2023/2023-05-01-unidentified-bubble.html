Issue 02 – May 23 – welcome to the New
It is 2053 and Plum is spending her days in her capsule/room with VTR set every single day and night. She has multiple rooms created in her virtual house, all inspired by a period between 1960s to 1980s, however being unsure how it all looked like decades and decades ago and having nobody to consult she imagines what it must have been like to live in Tokyo then. She has no clue, so she puts elements of one to the other, makes her own puzzle and her own - colored bubble. She sometimes listens to city pop, sometimes she doesn’t because she is unsure if the bubble is correct time reference.
