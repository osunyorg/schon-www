Issue 03 - the water issue
A creature of the depths, a siren of the sea who mesmerizes all who behold her. With skin as smooth as a pearl and eyes that shimmer like the ocean's depths, she glides effortlessly through the lagoon's azure waters. Her presence in the lagoon is like a retro splash, transporting observers to a simpler time, a world where style and elegance were everything. Those lucky enough to glimpse Lagoon Gal can't help but feel a sense of nostalgia for the days of vintage glamour and carefree fun. She is a siren of the sea, embodying the beauty and grace of a bygone era, a Retro Splash in the modern world.
