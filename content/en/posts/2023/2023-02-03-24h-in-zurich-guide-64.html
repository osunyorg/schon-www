Lunch at Bridge
Cost: between Chf10-30
After working up a voracious appetite from exercise, walk across the street to the food market Bridge. They have a wide selection of food choices and if your legs have already turned jelly from a strenuous workout, you can order and pay from the comforts of your table with their app and one of their wait staff will deliver your food and drinks to you. Art Therapy
Cost: From Chf59 for 2-3 hours
If sports is not your thing, perhaps you can book a guided painting experience at Paint Events. Their artist guides will help you step-by-step through a painting from their collection with different media like acrylic, watercolors or alcohol inks. You can also just come and pay for the material and create something of your own.
www.paintevents.ch (external link)

Try your hand in producing your own music
Cost: free
SAE Institute is a school for creative media studies like Game, Film, Music marketing and programming. They often hold free workshops that anyone of any age and level of digital experience can join like creating Game art and 3D animation, music production basics, photography workshops and also speed figure drawing classes where you get to sketch a model dressed in Anime costume and be guided by amazing mentors from the school.
www.sae.edu (external link)

Evening
Jazz and sunset sessions at Venice Bar
Cost: Chf12-50
After a fun-filled day of adventure, head to Venice Bar, a rooftop bar located at the Schiffbau building to watch the sunset and enjoy a nice cocktail and some venetian tapas. The bar has live jazz music every Thursday from 8pm onwards.
www.venicebar.ch (external link)

Thursday Jam
Cost: a small beer cost Chf5
If you are looking for somewhere more upbeat and rambunctious to end the night, head down to Amboss Rampe located next to the main train station, beside the big Zurich Brockenhaus second-hand shop. www.ambossrampe (external link).ch
