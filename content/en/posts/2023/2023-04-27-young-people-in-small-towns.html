Issue 02 – May 23 – welcome to the New
The story of young people living in small towns is also the story of everyone. When people grow up, they will encounter all kinds of growing pains, such as quarrels between intimate relationships, pressure of survival, peer pressure, and so on. This kind of dramatic change makes them breathless. So, they went back to the countryside when they were young, hugging, happy, running, and the breeze blew away, all the unhappiness disappeared, and only the beauty remained.
