Issue 01 – FEB/MAR 23 – In Love with Switzerland
Being immersed in the rhythm means being fully absorbed in the tempo, beat, or flow of music or movement. It creates a state of mind where you feel connected to the rhythm, which becomes an integral part of your experience. This feeling of flow can be achieved through various activities like dancing, singing, playing an instrument, or just listening to music. It is associated with happiness, joy, and relaxation as it reduces stress and anxiety. Being immersed in the rhythm is a rewarding and fulfilling experience that allows you to connect with music and movement.
